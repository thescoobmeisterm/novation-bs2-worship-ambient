## 🏛 The Seven Sonic Pillars (Slots 000–047)

### 1. Worship Foundation & Sub Bedrock (Slots 000–006)
- `Slot 000` — **Sanctuary Sub**: Pillowy, click-free sub-bass foundation below 60Hz. Operates with immense physical weight that shakes the room without clouding acoustic instruments or vocals.
- `Slot 001` — **Refinery Special**: *Signature church service bedrock!* Dual detuned saw waves layered with high-level sine sub, max pre-filter overdrive (127), and post-filter distortion.
- `Slot 002` — **Refinery Swell**: Prayer swell & ambient bloom evolution of Refinery Special. Warm ~1.2s attack bloom into lush analog harmonics for altar calls and spontaneous worship.
- `Slot 003` — **Anthem Drive Arp**: *Signature church service 16th arp!* (Originally crafted for the song *"Heaven"*). Syncopated 16th praise pulse with max overdrive.
- `Slot 004` — **Praise 16th Arp**: Resonant praise arp with keyboard tracking for soaring highs and a massive sub foundation.
- `Slot 005` — **Sunday Driver**: Dynamic, punchy P-bass synth with velocity bite.
- `Slot 006` — **Living Water**: Rubbery liquid bass with vocal portamento glide.

### 2. Post-Rock Power & Tectonic Textures (Slots 007–011)
- `Slot 007` — **Caspian Bow**: Bowed contrabass/cello slow swell with warm grit (*Caspian / GY!BE*).
- `Slot 008` — **Tectonic Dist**: Crushing high-gain fuzz with clean sub anchor (*Russian Circles*).
- `Slot 009` — **Mogwai Drone**: Paraphonic 2-voice heavy drone with PWM churn (*Mogwai*).
- `Slot 010` — **Sigur Lead**: Singing high lead using ACID diode ladder filter (*Sigur Rós*).
- `Slot 011` — **Quiet Earth**: Soft, felt-hammer melancholic intro bass (*Hammock*).

### 3. Ambient, Shimmer & Soundscapes (Slots 012–017)
- `Slot 012` — **Tape Drift**: Lo-fi reel-to-reel tape wow & flutter with slew drift (Osc Error 5).
- `Slot 013` — **Ether Swell**: 2.5-second ambient bloom pad for massive reverbs.
- `Slot 014` — **Glass Pluck**: Crystalline Band-Pass delay pluck for ambient arpeggios.
- `Slot 015` — **Aurora Waves**: Dual-LFO hypnotic texture with analog noise bed.
- `Slot 016` — **Hollow Chill**: Band-Pass 24dB scooped eerie background bed.
- `Slot 017` — **Celestial Lead**: Paraphonic ethereal dual-pulse singing lead/pad.

### 4. Cinematic Pulses & Rhythmic Ostinatos (Slots 018–023)
- `Slot 018` — **Sidechain Pump**: Pumping sidechain bass ducking behind kick drum.
- `Slot 019` — **Modular Bubble**: Sample & Hold liquid bubbling filter sequence.

### 5. Shoegaze Walls & Dream-Pop Textures (Slots 024–029)

## 🚀 Quick Install (Novation Components)

1. Connect your Novation Bass Station II to your computer via USB and open [Novation Components](https://components.novationmusic.com/) in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
2. Under **Librarian**, click **Upload Bank / Import** and select:
   `PostRock_Ambient_Worship_Bank.syx`
3. Click **Send to Bass Station II**. All 48 patches will instantly flash into Slots 000 through 047 on your hardware!
