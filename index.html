How to Flash into Your Bass Station II (Novation Components)

Novation Components is a free browser-based librarian that directly communicates with your Bass Station II over standard USB MIDI.

1

Connect Hardware

Connect your Novation Bass Station II to your computer using a standard USB-B cable and power the synth on.

2

Open Components

Open components.novationmusic.com in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.

3

Upload Bank

Select Bass Station II, click Upload Bank / Import, and select PostRock_Ambient_Worship_Bank.syx.

4

Send to Hardware

Click Send to Bass Station II. All 48 patches will instantly flash into Slots 000 through 047 on your synth!
