### Complete Patch Guide, 4-Lane Bank Organization, Tag Directory & Audio Samples

Welcome to the definitive manual for the **Novation Bass Station II (BSII) 48-Patch Library**.

This collection organizes **48 professional analog patches** into **4 distinct sonic lanes** of 12 patches each. In every lane, **sustained foundation & lead sounds come first**, followed immediately by **driving arpeggiators & rhythmic pulse engines**, making live service performance and setlist navigation effortless:

1. **Lane 1: Worship Foundation (Slots 000–011)**:
   - *Sustains (000–005)*: Deep sub bedrock, signature service dual-saw drive (`Refinery Special`), prayer room atmospheric swells (`Refinery Swell`), dynamic P-bass punch, and vocal portamento glides.
   - *Arpeggios (006–011)*: Your signature 16th-note praise arpeggiator (`Anthem Drive Arp`, originally crafted for *"Heaven"*), syncopated praise pulses, arena trance arps, cathedral plucks, and dotted-8th delay motors.
2. **Lane 2: Post-Rock (Slots 012–023)**:
   - *Sustains (012–020)*: Bowed cello swells, crushing fuzz monoliths, paraphonic drone churns, screaming diode ladder leads, felt melancholic intros, My Bloody Valentine glide guitar bends, 80s chorus bass, blackgaze walls of sound, and feedback squeals.
   - *Arpeggios (021–023)*: Explosive post-rock tremolo crescendos, shoegaze flutter strobes, and gritty cyberpunk drive sequences.
3. **Lane 3: Ambient (Slots 024–035)**:
   - *Sustains (024–032)*: Lo-fi tape drift with wow & flutter, 2.5s ether swells for stereo shimmer, crystalline glass plucks, aurora dual-LFO textures, hollow band-pass chill beds, paraphonic celestial leads, slowdive washes, vintage dream fluff, and analog transition risers.
   - *Arpeggios (033–035)*: Generative starlight bell constellations, FM modular bells, and delicate clockwork plucks.
4. **Lane 4: Funk & Gospel (Slots 036–047)**:
   - *Sustains (036–041)*: Dynamic envelope auto-wahs (Mu-Tron III vibe), gospel slap & chop bass, vocal formant talkbox leads, classic Bernie Worrell / Stevie Wonder mini funk, 303 acid squelch grooves, and nu-disco octave pumps.
   - *Arpeggios (042–047)*: Chromatic gospel shout arps, biting 303 acid arpeggios, driving octave gallops, liquid modular bubbles, vintage sequential Prophet plucks, and 3-against-4 sub polyrhythms.

#### 01. Sanctuary Sub
* **SysEx File**: [`01_Sanctuary_Sub.syx`](file:///C:/Users/dvgpr/Downloads/BS2_PostRock_Ambient_Worship_Patches/01_Sanctuary_Sub.syx) | **Bank Slot**: `000` | **Type**: Sustained Foundation / Lead
* **Audio Sample**: [01_Sanctuary_Sub.wav](file:///C:/Users/dvgpr/Downloads/BS2_PostRock_Ambient_Worship_Patches/samples/01_Sanctuary_Sub.wav)
* **Tags**: `sustain`, `sub`, `warm`, `bedrock`
* **What It Sounds Like**:
  Pure, deep, low-frequency foundation below 60Hz. Uses a pure sine sub-oscillator running 1 octave down with a low-pass 24dB Classic filter rolled off at ~140Hz. The attack is smoothed to 5ms to eliminate the transient key click that plagues budget synths.
* **Inspiration**: Moog Taurus bass pedals; Luke Anderson and David Funk foundational sub tracks (Bethel / Elevation).
* **Suggested Songs & Placement**:
  - *“Goodness of God”* (Bethel) — Chorus 2 and Bridge sustain.
  - *“The Blessing”* (Kari Jobe / Elevation) — Altar ministry accompaniment.
* **Performance Tip**: Mod Wheel opens the cutoff slightly (+25) if you need a touch more punch in the room.
